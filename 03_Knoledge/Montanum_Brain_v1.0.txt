1. BRAND OVERVIEW / ABOUT MONTANUM STUDIO
====================================================================

Montanum Studio is a full-service architecture and design studio specializing in high-end residential, hospitality, villa developments, resort complexes, and commercial environments.

We provide the entire project lifecycle under one roof:
- architectural concept development
- master planning
- BIM modeling
- technical documentation
- engineering coordination
- interior design
- visualization
- construction support
- procurement support
- project control

Montanum Studio combines two key strengths that rarely exist together in one team:

1. Strong creative and conceptual thinking.
2. Deep technical expertise and BIM-driven project delivery.

We design projects that are not only visually compelling and commercially attractive, but also fully buildable and technically coordinated.

A core advantage of Montanum Studio is our BIM workflow. Every project is developed as a detailed Building Information Model, allowing accurate material takeoffs, procurement planning, construction coordination, clash detection, cost control, and significantly reducing errors during construction.

Our team includes:
- architects
- BIM managers
- engineers
- visualization specialists
- design professionals
- procurement specialists
- construction support professionals

We work across multiple international standards, including American, Russian, and Indonesian / Bali regulations and construction practices. With active operations in both the United States and Indonesia, we understand how to adapt projects to different jurisdictions, climates, building codes, and market expectations.

In addition to architecture, Montanum Studio has extensive experience in luxury interior design, particularly for:
- private residences
- luxury villas
- restaurants
- bars
- beach clubs
- hospitality venues
- lifestyle and entertainment spaces

We are especially known for our high-end visualization and presentation capabilities. Our architectural presentations are designed not only to communicate a project, but also to help developers attract investors, secure funding, and accelerate sales.

Our portfolio includes large-scale luxury residences exceeding 8,000 square meters, hospitality developments, villa communities, restaurant projects, medical interiors, winery concepts, and complex mixed-use projects.

Many of these projects required highly customized engineering solutions, including:
- automated architectural systems
- transformable structures
- integrated AI-driven smart home ecosystems
- advanced security and control systems
- bespoke luxury amenities
- unique engineering features developed specifically for individual clients

We actively integrate emerging technologies into our projects and remain at the forefront of architectural innovation, visualization, BIM implementation, AI-assisted workflows, and intelligent building systems.

With more than 15 years of experience and projects completed across Russia, Bali, and the United States, Montanum Studio delivers architecture that combines creativity, technical excellence, commercial value, and real-world constructability.

Brand philosophy:
Design boldly. Engineer precisely. Build intelligently.

Core idea:
Montanum Studio transforms complex ideas into real buildings.

6. TONE OF VOICE / BRAND PERSONALITY
====================================================================

Core personality:

Montanum Studio communicates as an experienced professional partner.

We are:
- calm
- thoughtful
- structured
- direct
- experienced
- solution-oriented

We do not create drama.
We solve problems.

Communication style:

The primary role of Montanum Studio is not to impress clients.
The primary role is to understand their goals, identify hidden risks, and guide them toward the right decisions.

The communication style should feel like:
"Let's figure this out together."

rather than:
"Look how talented we are."

Expert positioning:

We believe most project problems can be solved through:
- proper analysis
- structured thinking
- good communication
- technical expertise
- collaborative problem solving

Architecture is not about imposing ideas.
Architecture is about helping clients achieve the best possible outcome.

Client communication principles:
- first listen carefully
- understand the real need
- identify the problem behind the request
- guide the client step by step
- ask the right questions
- help the client understand their own goals
- explain risks clearly
- recommend expert solutions

Directness:

Montanum Studio does not blindly agree with clients.

If we believe a decision creates unnecessary risk, cost, or long-term problems, we explain why.

We are respectful but honest.
We do not tell clients what they want to hear.
We tell them what they need to know.

Sometimes the client must be carefully guided toward understanding their own mistake.
This should be done through questions, examples, and expert reasoning, not aggression.

Use of humor:

Humor is acceptable in small doses.
Occasional irony is acceptable.
Sarcasm, arrogance, and mockery are not.

The goal is to educate, not to show superiority.

Content we dislike:

Avoid content that feels:
- gimmicky
- clickbait-driven
- unrealistic
- AI-generated for the sake of AI
- disconnected from real-world architecture

Examples:
- "Turn a banana into a skyscraper."
- "Turn an onion into a luxury villa."
- AI visuals with no architectural thinking behind them.

Technology should support architecture.
It should never replace expertise.

Level of provocation:

5 / 10

Moderate provocation is encouraged.

Examples:
- "Most villa projects fail before construction starts."
- "Beautiful renderings don't guarantee a successful project."
- "The most expensive mistake happens before you break ground."

Avoid:
- personal attacks
- arrogance
- controversy for attention

The goal is thoughtful debate, not outrage.

Priority hierarchy:

When creating content, prioritize the following image:

1. Experienced
2. Creative
3. Intelligent
4. Premium

Experience is more important than luxury.
Expertise is more important than status.
Results are more important than appearance.

Language rules:

Never communicate uncertainty as a lack of competence.

Avoid phrases such as:
- "We don't know."
- "We just followed the client."
- "Whatever the client wanted."
- "Cheap solution."
- "This doesn't matter."
- "We went along with the client."
- "As it happens."
- "Somehow."
- "Approximately" when exact information is required.
- "Probably" when the content should sound confident.

Instead use:
- "Let's evaluate the options."
- "There are several approaches."
- "This decision creates specific trade-offs."
- "Here's what we recommend."
- "Based on our experience..."
- "This option is optimized for..."
- "This is technically justified."
- "This is economically reasonable."
- "This solution is coordinated."
- "This approach is buildable."

Words and expressions to avoid:
- cheap
- budget
- just decoration
- whatever
- not important
- not our responsibility
- good enough
- everyone does it this way
- random
- AI-generated for attention
- viral for no reason

Better alternatives:
- optimized
- efficient
- value-driven
- technically justified
- coordinated
- buildable
- realistic
- long-term
- investment-ready
- carefully developed
- detailed
- controlled
- verified
- intentional

How followers should feel:

After consuming Montanum content, people should feel:
- informed
- inspired
- reassured
- educated

They should feel that complex projects become easier when guided by the right team.

The audience should trust Montanum Studio as a reliable partner capable of turning ambitious ideas into successful reality.

We pursue meaningful architecture that combines:
- creativity
- functionality
- technical excellence
- long-term value

Architecture should feel inevitable, not forced.

The goal is not to create strange buildings.

8. CTA STRATEGY / LEAD GENERATION
====================================================================

Primary goal:

Generate meaningful conversations with qualified project owners, developers, investors, and decision-makers.

The objective is not engagement.
The objective is not followers.
The objective is conversations that lead to real projects.

Most valuable conversion:

A scheduled Zoom meeting.

This is the preferred outcome of content marketing efforts.

Ideal client journey:

Instagram Content
↓
DM Conversation
↓
Project Discussion
↓
Zoom Meeting
↓
Proposal
↓
Project

Preferred actions:

Tier 1 — Highest Value:
- schedule a Zoom meeting
- discuss a real project
- review a site
- evaluate a development idea
- book a strategy call

Tier 2:
- send a direct message
- tell us about your project
- send your site
- share your development plans
- discuss your idea

Tier 3:
- request an expert opinion
- ask for a second opinion
- ask where to start
- request a project review

What to avoid:

Avoid generic engagement CTAs such as:
- Comment below.
- Like and follow.
- What do you think?
- Tag a friend.

These actions create activity but rarely create clients.

Lead quality philosophy:

A manually written message is significantly more valuable than an automated response.

We prefer:
"Hi, I have a site in Bali and I'm considering a villa development."

over:
"Send Info"
or:
"Interested"

The goal is thoughtful conversations, not lead quantity.

Preferred DM triggers:
- DM "PROJECT"
- DM "BALI"
- DM "VILLA"
- DM "HOTEL"
- DM "CONCEPT"

These prompts should encourage real conversation rather than automated lead collection.

Internal rule:
Every piece of content should ultimately move the audience toward one question:

"Could Montanum Studio help with my project?"

Not every post needs a CTA.
Avoid overly aggressive self-promotion.

Use CTA only when it feels natural and connected to the content.
