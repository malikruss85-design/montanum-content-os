# Image Generation Rules

## Primary rule

Use the supplied architecture as the source of truth.

Do not redesign the project unless the request explicitly asks for redesign.

## Lock hierarchy

1. architecture
2. master plan
3. camera
4. composition
5. materials
6. landscape
7. lighting and atmosphere
8. people and activity

## Standard constraints

- keep exact building geometry
- preserve relative building positions
- preserve camera height, lens and crop when requested
- do not add buildings
- do not remove architecture
- do not mirror the site
- do not invent extra floors
- do not change roof form unless specified
- modify only the named area
- maintain photorealistic architectural photography
- avoid obvious AI artifacts
- emotional realism over digital perfection

## Montanum editing pattern

Use Image 1 as the exact base.

Use additional images only as references for the specifically requested element.

State explicitly:

- what may change
- what must not change
- which reference supplies the replacement
- expected aspect ratio
- camera and composition constraints

## Common output formats

- 9:16 for Reels and Stories
- 4:5 for Instagram posts
- landscape hero images for presentations
