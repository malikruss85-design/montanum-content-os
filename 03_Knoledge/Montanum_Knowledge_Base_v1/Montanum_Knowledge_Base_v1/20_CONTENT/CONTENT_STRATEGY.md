# Content Strategy

## Priority

The content engine should first produce consistent, credible and visually strong founder-led content.

It is not intended to produce generic architecture inspiration.

## Core content pillars

- Project Showcase
- Projects and Process
- BIM and Architecture
- Development and Investment
- Visualization and Development
- Design Discovery
- Architect's Perspective
- Guest Experience
- Founder Thought
- Bali Development
- Materials and Detailing
- Construction and Coordination
- AI and Design Workflow
- Places, travel and observations
- founder commentary on locations, buildings and experiences

## Content goals

- demonstrate expertise
- show real projects
- explain decisions
- attract developers and investors
- attract hospitality and luxury residential clients
- show that Montanum combines design and implementation
- build recognition around Rustam's founder perspective
- create trust through process transparency

## Content test

Every post should answer at least one question:

- What did we design?
- Why was it designed this way?
- What problem does it solve?
- How does it improve value, operations or experience?
- What did we learn?
- What would an architect notice here?
