# Content Types

## Project Showcase

The most important format.

Show strong images and explain the project through design intent, development logic, commercial value or technical complexity.

## Founder Perspective

Short opinions and observations from Rustam.

Sources can include:

- active project decisions
- places visited
- hotels and restaurants
- material observations
- development mistakes
- architecture criticism
- AI workflow experiments
- construction realities

## Process Content

Examples:

- concept to BIM
- BIM to visualization
- empty site to final resort
- construction timelapse
- material selection
- procurement
- coordination
- problem and solution

## Educational Content

Explain architecture, BIM, hospitality planning and investment logic without becoming a generic tutorial account.

## Formats

### Reel

Hook, visual progression, founder insight and concise CTA.

### Carousel

One concept per slide, strong first slide, visual evidence, limited text.

### Story

Fast, informal and process-driven.

### Post

Useful when one hero image and a strong written idea are sufficient.

## Content bundle output

One good source bundle may produce:

- one Reel
- one carousel
- three to seven Stories
- one caption
- one LinkedIn variation
