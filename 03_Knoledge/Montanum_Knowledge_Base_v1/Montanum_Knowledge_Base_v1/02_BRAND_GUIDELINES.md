# Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Montanum Studio delivers full-cycle architecture and BIM-driven development services, from investment-ready concepts and visual storytelling to technical documentation, coordination, procurement support and project realization.

## Target clients

Primary:

- real-estate developers
- hospitality developers
- boutique-hotel and resort owners
- high-net-worth private clients
- investors
- restaurant, beach-club and lifestyle-venue owners

## Preferred project profile

Montanum is best suited to ambitious projects with meaningful architectural, technical or commercial complexity.

The studio generally does not pursue:

- decoration-only work
- small apartment renovations
- furniture-only selection
- commodity architecture
- isolated MEP work without full-project context
- low-budget residential work

## Geographic focus

- Bali and Indonesia
- United States, especially Los Angeles and Texas
- international clients and investors
- Russia and CIS where appropriate
- secondary focus on Europe and Middle East

## Language

Default public-content language: American English.

Use Russian when communicating with Russian-speaking clients or documenting internal discussion.

## Tone

- professional
- architect-level
- founder-led
- practical
- clear
- direct
- no hype
- no generic AI language
- minimal punctuation
- no unnecessary emojis
- commercially intelligent without sounding like advertising copy

## Preferred phrases

- From concept to reality.
- Architecture. BIM. Development.
- Original ideas. Intelligent design. Buildable architecture.
- We create architecture that is beautiful, buildable and commercially successful.
- We transform complex ideas into real buildings.
