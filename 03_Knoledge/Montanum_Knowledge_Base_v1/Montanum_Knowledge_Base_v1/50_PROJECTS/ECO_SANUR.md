# Eco Sanur / Eka Sanur

## Program

- main three-floor building, approximately 3,000 m²
- two three-floor hotel blocks, approximately 600 m² each
- four villas, approximately 150 m² each
- restaurant and lounge by the pool, approximately 600 m²

## Master-plan hierarchy

- front row: five one-floor villas
- behind: two larger two-floor villas
- rear: three- to four-floor main building with waterfall
- central ship restaurant

## Critical composition rules

- preserve the established master plan
- ship restaurant remains central
- beachfront and ocean relationship must remain legible
- roof forms are controlled and should not be improvised
- architecture is changed only when explicitly requested

## Visual-delivery goals

- five to six hero views
- day, evening and night variations
- rooftop bar
- vertical 9:16 content
- construction-timelapse sequence
- investor and sales presentation

## Known material and visual rules

- grey shingle roofs
- dark or aged-bronze fascia
- limestone large-format deck
- turquoise pool
- clean parapets
- controlled glass railings
- tropical but not chaotic landscaping
